IN THE TEMPLE

Opening: Watch the leader's sacrifice, Devil encounter, and Lilith's prayer. The crystal does not exist until Lilith places it. Walk: after the opening, hold right mouse to move and aim farther away to run. Left-click nearby objects to inspect. WASD / arrow keys also move. Ghosts pass through furnishings. Single-click the soul crystal to inspect; double-left-click to resurrect. Walking across it does nothing.

Build: The horizontal Ultima Online-style toolbar contains all 80 approved wall pieces, four automatic join finishes, floors, props and staging sprites. Choose a specific wall corner, end or junction to hand-place it; choose an auto-join finish to let neighboring walls decide their shape. The optional details drawer provides exact coordinates, import/export and advanced controls.

Exit: double-click the iron arch to open or close it. Walk into the open north doorway to enter Lilith's outdoor courtyard. Walking back through it returns to the temple.
