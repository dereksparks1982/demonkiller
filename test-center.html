IN THE TEMPLE

Opening: Watch the leader's sacrifice, Devil encounter, and Lilith's prayer. The crystal does not exist until Lilith places it. Walk: after the opening, hold right mouse to move and aim farther away to run. Left-click nearby objects to inspect. WASD / arrow keys also move. Ghosts pass through furnishings. Single-click the soul crystal to inspect; double-left-click to resurrect. Walking across it does nothing.

Build: choose Architect Mode, select art and click its grid coordinate. Select/Move uses two clicks. R rotates; Delete erases; Ctrl+Z undoes.

Exit: the northern doorway is a connection marker only. The courtyard is not yet built.
