# Demon Killer

**Demon Killer** is an engine-free, top-down Gothic fantasy adventure by **DK Softworks**, built with original pixel art. In the Forsaken Temple opening, the cult sacrifices the bound player, the Devil confronts the emerging ghost, and Lilith leaves a glowing soul crystal after praying at the altar. The cult leader, Devil, and Lilith speak in raised pixel speech bubbles with long, tapered pointers aimed toward the speaking character, keeping the boxes clear of the speaker and the body on the altar. The player can deliberately resurrect or remain a ghost. The sacrifice scene scales the lying body to 40×20 pixels on a centered three-by-two-tile altar (96×64 pixels), matching the smaller surrounding characters. DK Architect uses that same footprint for the translucent preview, rotation, placement, and collision checks.

The game includes character save slots and **DK Architect**, an in-game tool for building and editing the temple.

## Current temple audio and scene

The Forsaken Temple runs the owner-provided Lyon Organ Arrangement MIDI as a browser-synthesized pipe-organ loop when the music preference is enabled. Browsers may require a first click or key press before Web Audio can start. The temple uses a north door that starts closed, opens for the departing cult, closes after the last member, and stays open when Lilith leaves; the Devil vanishes in place. The music is **temporary development/reference material** originating in *Fire Emblem: The Sacred Stones* and must not be included in any commercial release without appropriate music rights or replacement with an original, licensed piece.

The approved NES portrait atlas and replacement temple wall atlas have been uploaded to their respective art folders, but the portraits still need splitting and the new wall tiles still need installing in place of the old ones. No new floor atlas has been made or requested in this build.
