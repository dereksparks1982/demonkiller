# Demon Killer

**Demon Killer** is an engine-free, top-down Gothic fantasy adventure by **DK Softworks**, built with original pixel art. In the Forsaken Temple opening, the cult sacrifices the bound player, the Devil confronts the emerging ghost, and Lilith leaves a glowing soul crystal after praying at the altar. The cult leader, Devil, and Lilith speak in character-anchored pixel speech bubbles during the ritual. The player can deliberately resurrect or remain a ghost. The sacrifice-scene composition uses the original 64×32 lying body and correctly oriented cult leader, Devil, Lilith, and cult members.

The game includes character save slots and **DK Architect**, an in-game tool for building and editing the temple.

## Current temple audio and scene

The Forsaken Temple runs the owner-provided Lyon Organ Arrangement MIDI as a browser-synthesized pipe-organ loop when the music preference is enabled. Browsers may require a first click or key press before Web Audio can start. The temple uses a north door that starts closed, opens for the departing cult, closes after the last member, and stays open when Lilith leaves; the Devil vanishes in place. The music is **temporary development/reference material** originating in *Fire Emblem: The Sacred Stones* and must not be included in any commercial release without appropriate music rights or replacement with an original, licensed piece.

The new NES portrait atlas and the approved replacement temple wall atlas still need their original image files imported; existing wall art is retained until the exact approved assets can be installed. No new floor atlas has been made or requested in this build.
