# M48 | Player-Owned Businesses and NPC Counter Service

Buy one of the five existing merchandise businesses from its living NPC proprietor at the actual storefront. The acquisition transfers real player gold to the NPC, preserving the company treasury and physical merchandise. Shop ownership belongs to the character and is separate from the `shop.owner_id` job-holder NPC used by schedules and succession.

Manage budget/standard/premium selling prices, the NPC assistant, automatic production, investment and owner draws (with minimum operating reserves), and physical stock deposits/withdrawals that preserve item serials. When a living player is physically at the counter, NPC sales are credited as player-served; while away, the real assistant serves instead, but only if present. Broader NPC role classes now make actual shopping trips and pay actual money.

The paper doll's BUSINESS panel exposes these controls, without adding buttons to the play screen. Only existing storefronts can be acquired; the approved village art and map layout remain unchanged. Character deletion releases shop ownership but preserves the marketplace, seller funds, assistants, stock and sale history. This is the beginning of deep business gameplay, not yet a full industry ownership simulator.
