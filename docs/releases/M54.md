# M54 | Physical Weapons, Armor, Ammunition and Equipment Durability

DK Server introduces an original **server-owned armory** layered onto the existing persistent item registry, without importing any RunUO GPL code or replacing approved pixel art.

## Physical equipment

Forge real short swords, maces, spears, bows, bucklers, iron armor and arrows using wood, ore and herbs already collected in the game. Metalwork requires the existing village smithy; crafting consumes precisely the required items and trains the associated UO-style skill. Forging is limited to one work action per simulated world minute. Bows use physical arrows, and only shoot in unobstructed cardinal lines within five tiles.

A durable armory has three equipment slots: weapon, offhand, armor. Equipped items retain their **original serial identity** and physical location inside unique one-slot world containers. Server combat uses the actual equipped weapon's damage and Swordsmanship / Mace Fighting / Fencing / Archery skill. Bucklers can parry, while armor reduces incoming monster damage. Weapon strikes, blocked hits and armor absorption wear the equipment. Reaching zero condition destroys the physical item and removes its authoritative slot reference.

Durability also lives on the **item serial**, not only the character. Taking off a damaged weapon and putting it back on cannot reset its wear. Old hand tools and a new weapon are mutually exclusive through either inventory path. Persistent bank storage, backpack transfer, item conservation, unique entity serials, and character retirement respect the new equipment types and slots. Retirement cannot leave the old character's equipment behind for a new character to inherit.

## Interface and limitations

The classic paper doll gains **ARMORY** for equipment, forging, arrows, current durability, and equip/unequip actions. It has no extra gameplay HUD button and no new unapproved sprites. This slice adds the foundation for wearable gear, but is not a claim that a complete medieval smithing/mining/armor appearance/repair economy exists. At this stage equipment **breaks permanently** at zero durability; repair services and a deeper armorer profession can be built later.

## Source and verification

- `server/armory-m54.cjs` provides ownership, forging, wear, defense and read-only listing.
- `server/entity-registry-m10.cjs` conserves gear serials and recognizes equipment containers.
- `server/combat-m8.cjs` handles real weapons, ranged sight lines, ammunition and defensive gear.
- `tests/armory-m54.cjs` exercises crafting, equipment, durability persistence, arrows, armor, breaking and retirement.

Isolated functional simulation with the real entity-registry implementation passed 11 checks. Full local browser/server regression tests are pending; GitHub Actions must **not** run automatically on push.
