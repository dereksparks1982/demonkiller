# Demon Killer M51 | Actual Owner-Served NPC Customers

An NPC who travels to a player-owned storefront while the living owner is physically behind its counter now waits for an explicit Serve Customer interaction instead of being automatically sold goods. A saved customer order records the NPC, shop, arrival world minute and a thirty-minute wait deadline.

Serving validates ownership, proximity, a real live customer and existing purchase goal. The same physical vendor item serial transfers to the NPC's personal backpack, exactly the proper money moves from NPC to business, and sales are credited to the owner. Duplicate/forged service requests are rejected. Unserved customers leave after thirty running world minutes with neither money nor goods lost. If the owner leaves, a living assistant at the store may automatically complete the queued transaction.

The paper doll's BUSINESS panel lists waiting customers and has a Serve Customer command. Old M50 world saves acquire an empty pending-order store non-destructively. Character deletion clears the retired character's pending orders but leaves NPC customers and their underlying shopping plans in the persistent world. Original world art and geometry remain untouched.
