# Demon Killer M45 | Town Bounties

Four physical monster contracts issued by existing village and courtyard businesses. A living player must visit the issuer to accept and later claim; only player-caused kills count. Rewards are deducted from the business's actual purse and recorded in the persistent world. Contract state and kill history are validated on reload.

The paper doll's QUESTS panel now reads and operates this actual ledger. It is a first quest family, not an invented full faction-guild system.
