# M50 | Manufacturing Breakdowns and Real Repairs

Production causes wear to existing bakery, smithy and tavern equipment. Reaching 100 wear breaks the machine after that completed batch and blocks further manual or automatic manufacturing. Monster raids can also damage machines. Shops may still sell their already-existing merchandise if staffed, but cannot produce fresh goods with broken equipment.

A player-owner can travel to the damaged station and consume one real spare tool to repair it immediately (Tinkering skill growth), or order an NPC worker to repair it. Contract repair money is reserved from the actual shop treasury and delivered to the physically present worker only after 60 running world minutes. If the owner uses tools during an outstanding order, the reserved money is returned. If a repair worker dies or cannot reach the station, the job remains pending, not magically completed.

NPC-owned businesses may autonomously fund repairs after downtime when a living employee is at the station. World changes, failures and completed repairs are persistent and written into the event history. The existing BUSINESS panel displays machine condition and repair options. The approved town geometry and art are unchanged.
