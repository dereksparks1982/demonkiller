# Demon Killer M52 | Hiding, Stealth Walking and Camping

UO-style Hide now connects to a limited Stealth movement mode. After successfully hiding, choose Stealth from the existing SKILLS paper-doll panel. The number of legal cardinal steps scales with trained Stealth skill, and world-owned monster proximity can reveal the character. The movement validator still enforces all original region boundaries, portals, solid objects and no diagonal movement. Exhaustion/detection are recorded in world events, and actual sneaking trains Stealth.

Camping is another real field skill, available to a conscious living character on safe outdoor ground with one actual wood in inventory and no dangerous monsters within range. Using it consumes wood and restores bounded health and energy; the camp duration and the character's fieldcraft state are saved with the world. Leaving camp or moving breaks the rest state.

M43 fieldcraft saves migrate non-destructively by adding optional stealth-step and camp-until fields; the world loader accepts the old shape before runtime migration. Existing artwork and approved temple/Lilith behavior are not modified.
