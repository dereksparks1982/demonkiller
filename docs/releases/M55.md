# M55 | The Village Armorer: Real Repairs, Contracts and Salvage

The existing village smithy now runs a persistent **equipment-repair service**, extending M54 physical weapon and armor durability. Every job keeps the unique original item serial. Nothing is magically returned to the player's inventory when a timer finishes.

## Work it yourself

While physically at the smithy with hand tools equipped, a conscious living player can repair damaged, unequipped equipment using **one real ore** (or wood for a bow). The material is consumed; the original serial retains its identity and returns to full durability. The associated Blacksmithing/Carpentry skill receives legitimate progression. Manual smith work has a world-minute cooldown. A player can also dismantle one physical sword, mace, spear, bow, shield or iron armor for a single ore/wood item. This destroys the original gear serial and always produces less raw material than the original recipe consumed.

## Hire an actual smith

A character at the open smithy can drop off damaged gear for **12 gold**. The payment leaves their purse and enters the actual smithy treasury. The original physical item is moved into the smith's durable work-order container, not copied or kept in the player backpack. The shop issues a persistent job ID with a **60 world-minute earliest finish**.

Completion requires a living smith at the physical station, shop hours, safe settlement control, functioning smithy machinery, enabled assistant service when the shop has a player owner, at least one genuine ore/wood from the business's existing vendor inventory, and enough company cash to pay **three gold wages**. Without these conditions, the job remains pending beyond its nominal due time. Once completed, the NPC consumes the actual material serial, earns actual pay, and moves the **same repaired equipment serial** to a separate pickup container. It remains there until the character visits and collects it, with capacity checks and no duplication.

Character retirement removes uncollected personally owned repair orders and their items but preserves the smithy, treasury, independent NPC workers, world and business history. Existing M54 worlds migrate by adding the repair ledger and two registered work-order containers.

## Controls and limits

The ARMORY paper-doll section shows the smith's condition, raw supply and outstanding jobs, with **repair by hand**, **leave with smith**, **collect**, and **salvage** commands. No extra on-screen play controls or new artwork have been introduced. This is a first profession service, not yet a general item-quality, enchantment, auction or player-to-player crafting economy.
