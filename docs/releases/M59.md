# M59 | Persistent creature spawning and regional ecology

### Server design
- Four saved regional controllers with stable IDs, due-minute schedules, population limits, number-created counters and last processed tick. No client spawn commands.
- Immediate occupation waves preserve M8 behavior, with 90-minute rotating reinforcements while the territory remains occupied.
- Threat-gated wolf/bandit wilderness presence on a 120-minute clock when the area is governed by settlers.
- Clearing every occupying creature keeps the former territory liberation rule but establishes a persisted 120-minute grace period. No immediate fake respawn if monsters reclaim territory.
- Server-authoritative collision-safe spawn locations avoid other creatures, living villagers and player.
- Legacy-save migration is non-destructive and idempotent. Invalid ledgers block save validation rather than resetting world records.
- `GET /api/v1/spawners`: read-only world status for development and diagnostics.
- No new visuals, gameplay buttons, temple or Lilith modifications. The existing creature species, gear, business, corpse and loot systems remain in place.
