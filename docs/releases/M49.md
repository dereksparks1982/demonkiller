# M49 | Personally Operated Workshops

A player who owns the Village Bakery, Smithy or Tavern can manually run the actual production station while standing by its storefront. Each transaction consumes physical vendor-stock ingredients and produces a new durable item serial in the business's stock. The bakery converts sacked flour into bread, the smithy converts ore/wood into tools, and the tavern converts produce into meals.

One manual production action per world minute prevents instant duplication. Cooking/Blacksmithing skill increases are validated by the server. The NPC assistant can continue production while the owner is away, unless the owner turns off automatic production or the assistant's service. No wage is fabricated for player manual labor. Real vendor item counts, business stock and world events remain consistent.
