# Demon Killer M44 | Mana, Spellbook and Reagents

Five castable skills-gated, server-authoritative spells: Magic Arrow, Fireball, Heal, Protection and Night Sight. Mana regenerates on server-world minutes. Herbal reagents are consumed; offensive spells require straight north/east/south/west line of sight, spend mana and obey real cooldowns. A kill from offensive magic goes through the monster's durable corpse/loot code and can earn bounty credit.

The paper doll has a clickable spellbook; the player must be connected to DK Server. No spell bypasses the temple's soul-crystal resurrection.
