# Demon Killer M30 - The World Remembers

**DK Server 2.10.0**

## Server systems

- Carrion animals are persistent world mobiles. Ravens visit early human remains, rats visit later decay, and beetles gather around demonic bodies.
- Each scavenger has a stable entity serial, saved tile position, associated corpse, arrival time, and visit state. Encounters are determined by world minutes, not browser timers or random client behavior.
- Each ordinary corpse receives at most two scavenger visits. Visitors are capped, and expired or buried remains immediately disperse associated animals.
- Corpse records remember whether they have been disturbed by scavengers. M29 saves migrate safely without resetting existing bodies or bags.
- Players can bury nearby human remains with tools and sufficient energy. This transaction removes the original corpse and any buried possessions without awarding coins or creating duplicate items.
- Graves record the deceased person's name, exact tile, death reference, time of burial and permanent grave identity. Fresh mounds settle and eventually become overgrown while the world runs.
- Graves and scavengers use the same persistent entity registry and spatial-sector index as the rest of the world. New world data remains backward compatible with older saves.
- The existing single-writer save queue, world scheduler, server authority, and item serial accounting are retained.

## Gameplay

- **B** near a human body offers to bury it after confirmation. No visible gameplay shortcut button was added.
- Graves, ravens, rats and beetles are drawn as small procedural pixel shapes on the outdoor terrain.
- Left-clicks inspect graves and scavengers; P, I and C continue to work as before.
- No changes were made to the approved hold-right-mouse movement algorithm or walking interpolation.

## Tests

- `tests/death-ecology-m30.cjs`: migration, carrion visits, species, burial, repeated actions, grave aging, permissions, timed jobs, sector identities and HTTP restart.
- `tests/death-ecology-ui-m30.cjs`: renderer ordering, keyboard-only controls and sector integration.
- Updated M11 scheduler expectations to include the new timer jobs, and aligned older server-version checks.
- Focused functional checks of corpse disturbance, two scavenger visits, item-safe burial and world serials have been performed. **The complete Node.js test suite has not been confirmed as passing.**

## Boundaries

Single-player first. No RunUO code, third-party sprites, external art packs or game engine were imported. These are independently written server systems.
