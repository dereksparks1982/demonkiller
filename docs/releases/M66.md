# M66 | Real-material reconstruction after siege

**Scope:** One substantial server-authoritative rebuilding build, continuing M61–M65 settlement defense, occupation, evacuation and recovery. No UI buttons or art changes.

## World behavior
- Occupation now damages two deterministic village buildings or one courtyard site; structural condition is 0–100 damage and is persisted by existing business ID.
- Real structures must be repaired before a previously invaded settlement can finish its M65 campaign recovery.
- Damaged businesses stay closed. NPC workshop production, manual operations and milling cannot use structurally destroyed buildings, even if machinery is otherwise operational.
- Up to one construction contract per area, assigned to a real NPC. The construction worker visits the existing area's source storefront, checks active staffing, stock, price and treasury, and checks out two wood and one tool by physical serial into an NPC backpack.
- Construction requires walking to the actual site, then 30 uninterrupted simulated minutes at work. Each successful job consumes carried material and repairs 25 damage points.
- Purchasers pay vendor price (respecting player price mode), and six gold per job is paid to the actual worker on completion. Source shop, target treasury, equipment/production economy, item serials and historical events remain in sync.
- Harvesting takes priority when the blacksmith still has an active resource supply journey. Wood and tools require the existing harvesting, crafting, trade or player-procured stock. The courtyard cannot conjure imports; players can deliver materials through existing shop stock management.
- Occupation, insufficient materials or cash, absent/dead employees, missing cargo, failed routing, and player assistants switched off can interrupt construction. Lost carriers do not generate free cargo recovery; future workers must procure fresh material.
- Saved damage/work orders migrate old worlds non-destructively, retain all existing characters, purchases, corpses and area modifications, and validate strictly.
