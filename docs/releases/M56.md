# M56 | Real Carcass Harvesting and the Hunting-to-Food Economy

DK Server v2.31.0 extends the existing M12/M29 **persistent corpse-as-terrain** system. A slain creature still leaves one physical corpse that changes through its ordinary decay stages. Harvesting does **not** despawn or replace the body, create a second artificial loot corpse, or reset decay.

## Field work and usable resources

While alive, conscious, equipped with existing hand tools and physically within two tiles of an eligible corpse:

- A fresh or early-decay **wolf** can be skinned once for **two hides**, and butchered only while fresh for **two raw meat**.
- A fresh or early-decay **giant spider** can be skinned once for **two spider silk**, and have **one venom** extracted using an actual clean bandage.
- Spider bodies cannot provide wolf meat; human and demon remains cannot be butchered through this mechanic.
- Once a body has aged past its usable stages, extraction fails. Separate booleans for skin, butcher, and venom are stored **on that original corpse**, not in a disposable client collection; future saves and resurrections cannot duplicate goods.

The world clock enforces **one field-work action per world minute**, including camp/tavern cooking. Real hides, meat, silk and venom receive item serials and appear in existing pack/bank/vendor storage without violating capacity. Extracting resources marks the existing corpse as disturbed, without removing it. Training uses the existing Animal Lore, Cooking, and Poisoning professions.

## Cook and trade

At an active outdoor camp (M52) or the physically open Village Tavern, players may cook **one raw meat plus one wood** into one edible meal. The supplies are consumed and a real meal is created. The tavern buys raw meat using company funds; the village marketplace buys hides, meat and spider silk; the courtyard herb merchant buys spider silk and venom.

When actual raw meat and produce are stocked at the tavern, its physically present worker or its player-owner's manual production action uses **one meat plus one produce** to make a meal. When meat is unavailable, the old **two-produce** recipe remains. NPC production still requires normal M20 staffing, funds and usable machinery. This introduces a real player-to-NPC business supply path: hunt, butcher, transport, sell, bake/cook, then NPCs purchase the meals.

## Changes and verification

`server/hunting-m56.cjs` handles server-owned harvesting, cooking, records, world-minute limits, and listing. Existing M12/M29 corpse identity and decay remain the sole authority; older saves get optional harvest flags without deleting bodies. `tests/hunting-m56.cjs` includes world-level regression contracts, and the actual entity-registry simulation passed 13 isolated checks. A real DK Server is required for the gameplay; GitHub Pages is a static preview. Full browser/server tests are not claimed.

Approved sprites, Lilith's tree states, temple opening, DK Architect, village dimensions and the classic movement controls are unchanged. GitHub Actions remains **manual-only**.
