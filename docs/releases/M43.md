# Demon Killer M43 | Living Field Skills

Real server actions: tracking nearby mobiles, hiding only when safe, examining anatomy, forensic examination of persistent remains, meditation to restore mana, sensing spirits and studying animal health/loyalty. Anti-repeat cooldowns and a short concealment state are saved in the character. Movement and attacking cancel hiding; enemies respect it.

Not yet implemented: fishing waters, musical instruments, lockable chests and many other professional targets. Skill names are not claims of finished gameplay.
